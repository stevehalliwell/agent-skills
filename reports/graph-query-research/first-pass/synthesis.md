# Graph-query research — first-pass shortlist

## Result

Five lanes converge on a practical direction: **discover explainable patterns across the graph, then make supporting relationships easy to see**. Do not lead with an opaque importance/similarity score, automatic design advice or a universal layout.

These are proposed candidates, not adopted algorithms or implementation-ready tasks. Parent read all five reports, extracts, candidate CSVs and journals. Public quotations were not independently re-fetched. No graph-wide analysis, performance benchmark, player study or comprehension test ran.

## 1. Explainable graph-wide discovery — leading analysis candidate

Use separate typed relation lenses to discover shared ingredients, short indirect paths and shared prerequisite foundations. Generate candidates automatically; pair inspection follows discovery. Preserve relationship type and direction rather than flattening everything into one network.

Concrete inspection examples from authored plumber records:

- Discover jump-stomp and fall-stomp as a family through shared above-impact understanding and walker-stomp mechanic.
- Traverse farther to reveal stop-at-edge as an indirect shared foundation, through different landing/falling branches.
- Distinguish direct common prerequisites, indirect shared foundations, and each branch's extra AND requirements. Preserve proposed status.
- Discover first-gap/final-gap through shared required jumping, while exposing a changed role for target landing rather than calling the two challenges equivalent.

Visible result: selectable families/candidate groups, shared trunks and different branches; supporting typed paths/source lines on selection. Separate derived connectors from authored links. Show feature prevalence, query scope and unknown/omitted results. A common hub should not silently dominate every finding.

Source basis: [similarity report](02-similarity-discovery/first-blush/answer-analyst.md), including [PathSim](https://vldb.org/pvldb/vol4/p992-sun.pdf) and [GDS node-similarity documentation](https://neo4j.com/docs/graph-data-science/current/algorithms/node-similarity/). Typed postings and local adaptations are proposals, not demonstrated game-design results. Top-k display limits alone do not bound candidate-generation work.

## 2. Shared foundations and declared combinations — complementary analysis candidate

Find transitive foundations and explicit AND convergence across the corpus. Distinguish separately offered constituent skills from opportunities involving the actual combination.

Crate example: coordinate combines plan-push AND route-switch. Trace both branches, show where coordinate is demanded, and inspect existing earlier-opportunity/bypass witnesses. Do not infer that a combination is difficult, a bypass is wrong, or an introduction was learned.

Visible result: AND junction cue, distinct prerequisite branches, graph-wide finding badges and inspectable supported/bypass witnesses. Existing native route demand closure/opportunity checks already supply much of the analysis; proposed value is discovery, combination distinction and clearer presentation, not another route engine.

Sources: [structure report](01-structure-dependencies/first-blush/answer-analyst.md); [application report](03-game-design-applications/first-blush/answer-analyst.md); [Refraction progression paper](https://centerforgamescience.org/files/assets/papers/chi2015_progression.pdf). Its solution graphlets require actual solution representations; authored prerequisite motifs are not equivalent. Refraction findings do not establish general learning benefit or superiority of generated progression.

## 3. Route dominators — distinct later analysis candidate

Find challenges that necessarily precede a target in the declared route projection; expose bypassable practice and reconvergence. Crate push-room precedes combined-room on both authored branches, while switch-practice is bypassable.

Visible result: mandatory-prefix overlay retaining original alternatives and supporting declarations. Do not replace route graph with a tree silently. Entry/exit policy, unknown links, cycles and synthetic roots must remain explicit. This says nothing about actual traffic, physical reachability or teaching success.

Source basis: [structure report](01-structure-dependencies/first-blush/answer-analyst.md) and [rooted dominator documentation](https://networkx.org/documentation/stable/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.dominance.immediate_dominators.html). Original algorithm/implementation choice needs follow-up. Degree/betweenness are exploration measures, not substitutes for mandatory-route analysis.

## Drawing and interaction requirements across candidates

- Match drawing to task: aligned flow for prerequisites/routes; preserve branch/rejoin and explicit cycle/backlink exceptions. Rank, entry-hop bands and physical progression are not interchangeable.
- Prioritize traceable paths and avoid edges passing under unrelated nodes/labels. Minimizing global crossings is not the sole objective; extra bends can harm continuity.
- Use text/shape/pattern as well as colour for relation meaning, uncertainty and analysis state. Keep qualified identities inspectable; do not hide different identities behind identical truncated labels.
- Label grouping basis. Geometric proximity alone must not imply similarity, shared pool identity or a new design taxonomy.
- Finding selection visibly changes graph. Keep context by default; make hiding separate and reversible. Compact legend/layer toggles should show active scope and assumptions.
- Let users inspect both supporting and bypass/counterexample witnesses. Mark retained paths, truncation and unknown scope; one shown path is not all routes.
- Keep typed context expansion explicit. Exploration history, if needed later, stays separate from edit undo and source snapshots.
- Dense graphs may need a matrix/aggregate overview plus node-link detail. Existing noun/verb matrix is not an already implemented prerequisite matrix. No universal node-count cutoff, font optimum or one best layout established.

Sources: [visual-exploration report](04-visual-exploration/first-blush/answer-analyst.md), [drawing report](05-perceptual-drawing/first-blush/answer-analyst.md). Strong empirical passages: [Ware et al. path tracing](https://www.cs.kent.edu/~jmaletic/cs63903/papers/Ware02.pdf), [Ghoniem et al. representation comparison](https://mohammad.ghoniem.info/research/ivs-usability-2005.pdf). Their abstract/generated-graph tasks do not establish effectiveness for our game-design corpus. Software manuals establish interaction precedent, not comprehension benefit.

## Prerequisite integration work

All lanes identified a real distinction between declared data and consumed native edges. Challenge schemas include mechanics/interactions/nouns/verbs/resources, but current native challenge parsing consumes skill-role links and next, not all those context fields. Resource initial/capacity links likewise do not automatically enter generic native graph.

Do not compensate by asking authors to repeat indirect links. Refine reader coverage and projection semantics before claiming full encounter/resource discovery. Skill-only discovery has a narrower existing data base. Preserve source/status identity, AND semantics, drafts/undo, stale/unsafe-reload protection and source inspection.

## Defer rather than overclaim

- Embedding-first similarity and mandatory 2D projection: no demonstrated benefit over explainable lenses yet; supporting-path explanation still needed.
- Shape signatures/community partitions: secondary exploratory lenses, sensitive to labels, incompleteness and resolution; no natural game taxonomy established.
- Automatic tutorial/goal chains: [AtDelfi](https://arxiv.org/html/1807.04375v2) supplies precedent and map-necessity failures. Local terminal, geometry and event semantics remain incomplete.
- Resource balancing/simulation: [GEEvo](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.18574v1) requires executable economics/objectives. Pool bindings, occurrence identity, scenarios and action counts cannot be invented from topology. Numeric objective satisfaction can erase intended mechanics.
- Persistent exploration history and general all-pair cuts: uncertain value relative to simpler visible discovery/witness inspection.

## Recommended next review

Refine one bounded discovery capability and its visible result using the existing examples; coordinate drawing/legend improvements with viewer tasks. Establish what a designer must be able to find and explain before selecting scores, layouts or dependencies.

If stronger evidence is needed first, request a deeper pass focused on selected techniques, original algorithm/drawing papers or missing application examples. No deeper research campaign or implementation follows automatically from these provisional reports.
