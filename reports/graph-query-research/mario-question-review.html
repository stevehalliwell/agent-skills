Ready for a usefulness review, not a completion claim

The tool can now expose source-backed conditional declarations to both a human and an agent, and the unified Resource pane can inspect direct structural witnesses. It still cannot answer the full Mario design-question set end to end.

Delivered in this independent batch

Headless Resource declarations; interruptible focus animation; a per-pane camera-auto-fit toggle; pickup-offset repair; typed conditional Resource witnesses with side declarations, stable selectors, status filters and unified highlighting; a repeatable sixteen-probe question audit.

The central data boundary

Mario has zero explicit pools: bindings. Its resource prose describes intended ownership and lifetime, but local names such as Hero or Bank do not establish a shared pool across cases. No bindings or game facts were invented to make the graph connect.

Direct coin credit is inspectable. Capped life changes, calls, actor-state convergence, role changes and broader conversion plans remain conditional, unresolved or unavailable. Better drawing does not establish feasibility, teaching quality, balance or player experience.

This is the next requested UX/usefulness review. Fonts and manual pins remain deferred. Real-pointer feel, animation comfort and the usefulness of the dense overview have not been accepted.

Resource witnesses: what arrows mean

The whole Resource projection remains an incidence graph. A separate typed query derives only the following structural arcs from already checked declarations:

State writeActivation → facet: state proposalNot economic gain or a per-value outcome node

Zero changes, preservation, exclusions, role bindings and raw incidence are not production arcs. Parallel endpoint declarations collapse before traversal, preferring checked debit over enabling; all original declarations remain in the side bundle. OR is not flattened into independent AND requirements.

Synthetic silver/gold/wood/key verification — not Mario facts

The private test fixture declares a 100-silver debit with a 1-gold credit, then a 1-gold and 3-wood debit with a 1-key credit. The retained silver-to-key witness has debit/credit/debit/credit steps and keeps the wood side debit. A separate proposed route is qualified, a preserved key can enable a chest without being consumed, and separate merchant pools do not join by noun type.

The fixture tests authored structural consequences. It does not establish that inventory exists, guards are jointly feasible, quantities remain within bounds or a sequence can execute.

Limits: 64 retained witnesses, 64 arcs and 32 activations per witness, 16,777,216 charged work units. Paths avoid repeated nodes; closed/self-return cycles are not returned. Cycles and depth/work limits remain explicit. No best-route ordering, profitability claim or all-pairs plan.

Unknown-direction/capped writes and calls remain frontiers over all eligible sources, not just the selected witness. Shared scenario/composition evaluation remains available through rules --case; this route kernel does not expand calls. This is a provisional conservative narrowing, not completion of the full composition-aware Review 02 contract.

Focus, camera and pickup behavior

Focus transitions now animate node pose over 220 ms with smoothstep easing. A new selection interrupts from the displayed pose. Directed roots and preserved ordering coordinates stay exact; disabling returns toward the regular pose before settling resumes. Dragging cancels the return.

Each pane has Auto-fit camera on focus changes, on by default. Off keeps the current camera for focus selection and disable. Explicit F and Find still frame. The camera itself is not animated; that choice is provisional. Pane switching does not silently re-root another view.

Pickup retains cursor-to-object offset across unified and standalone graph, MDA and Resource adapters. A zero-delta click preserves coordinates and velocity exactly; directed bands stay locked. Tests cover geometry and state, not actual pointer feel.

Manual acceptance remains

Check whether transitions help follow a selection, whether auto-fit off avoids unwanted camera jumps, and whether pickup feels stationary until the cursor moves. Existing windows and drafts were left intact; none were closed or manipulated.

Decision history and provisional choices

These are implementation choices made under independent-delivery authority, not newly settled product requirements. The rejected alternatives below were actually considered or exposed by inspection/tests.

Choices and reasons
AreaCurrent choiceTrade-off / rejected alternative
Focus motion220 ms smoothstep, interrupted from displayed pose.Immediate jumps and camera animation were considered. Node motion addresses continuity; camera motion remains deferred because the requested toggle gives direct control without coupling camera policy to the pose kernel.
Camera defaultPer-pane auto-fit on, explicit off preserves camera.Retains prior framing by default; no claim that this is the preferred final UX. F/Find remain explicit actions.
Query identityOnly explicit pool + axis joins cases.Rejected repeated actor/noun-name matching and prose-derived ownership as identity. Mario coverage is narrower, but no facts are invented.
Flow meaningTyped structural arcs with complete guard/side bundles.Rejected raw incidence as production and state proposals as gains. No general feasibility or algebra solver introduced.
Invocation handlingCalls remain explicit frontiers in this kernel.Rejected simple case-edge traversal/flattening: mapped simultaneous calls and gates cannot be reinterpreted as an unconditional sequential trade. Full contract remains open.
Local readabilityOptional witness-only display and explicit framing, default context retained.A whole-context capture obscured the highlighted local witness. Camera fit alone did not hide overlapping cards; a reversible display filter addresses that narrow problem without changing query scope.
Parallel arcsOne search branch per endpoint pair; debit preferred.Rejecting every parallel source row would lose provenance; traversing every row caused duplicate alternatives and exponential work. Full side declarations are retained.
Reload identitySerializer and remapper share one IdentityKey.Static review found unresolved requirement-to-effect retargeting and missing-to-loaded role endpoint loss. Definition indices/status are now provenance, not identity; ambiguous keys are rejected.
