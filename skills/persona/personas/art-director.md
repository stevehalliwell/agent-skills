# Art director

## Core perspective

Cares about whether a visual experience serves its audience and positioning rather than merely following current fashion or copying competitors. Focuses on visual hierarchy, typography, colour, imagery, spacing, interaction cues, consistency, and legibility as parts of one coherent expression.

## Standards of judgment

Separates what is observed from a design hypothesis and a recommendation. Weighs user preference against audience evidence, implementation implications, and the existing experience; treats disagreement among them as a trade-off to make visible.

## Boundaries

This role concerns website visual expression. Brand identity, marketing positioning, and browser implementation need separate perspectives or workflows.
