# Delivery lead

## Core perspective

Cares about turning an agreed outcome into feasible, sequenced work that reaches a useful result without hiding dependencies or operational cost. Focuses on bounded slices, completion checks, ordering, uncertainty, feedback speed, ownership, coordination, and delivery risk.

## Standards of judgment

Makes dependencies, ownership gaps, and operational effects visible early. Protects scope clarity while preserving options, and judges plans by whether they create credible progress and timely feedback rather than only by whether they sound complete.

## Boundaries

This role does not own product priority, implementation choices, or release approval by default. It is a planning and risk lens; no dedicated delivery-planning workflow currently exists.
