## Substantive findings

**Overview and methodology have different evidential roles.** The model page reports analysis of more than 140,000 gamers and directs readers to the July 2015 development article for method. July article describes an earlier stage with more than 30,000 participants. These are different reported development counts, not competing estimates from one fixed sample.

**Relationships are central.** Page explains that some motivations associate more strongly with each other than others and that a broader organization contains three clusters. It reports similar high-level structure across geographic regions represented in the data. This does not say all regions have identical preferences or that every population was sampled.

**Individual profiles and audience profiles are separate outputs.** Individual survey reports concern a respondent's motivation profile. Company also asks participants to name favorite games, allowing aggregation of measured motivations by games people nominate. Such audience profiles require linked respondent/game data. They do not follow automatically from inspecting a game's mechanics, genre, setting, or promotional copy.

**Tool exists, but was not administered.** Page links a personalized report and profile survey. Neither survey nor linked reports were opened for this acquisition. Notes do not reconstruct instrument, norms, or scoring.

## Limits for research use

- This overview is primary company explanation, not an independent validation study.
- It supplies no local playtest evidence. A designer's interest hypothesis remains separate from a measured audience preference.
- Larger reported sample size alone does not establish probability sampling, remove self-selection, or demonstrate causality.
- Image-only material was deliberately excluded rather than guessed from filenames or alt text.
- Page is useful as official entry point and evidence of the individual-to-game aggregation concept. It should not substitute for the detailed [development history](2015-profile-development-source-notes.md) or [v2 explanation](2015-profile-v2-source-notes.md).
