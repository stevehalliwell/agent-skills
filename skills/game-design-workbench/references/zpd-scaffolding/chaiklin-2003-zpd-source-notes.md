## Main argument

1. **Development is not interchangeable with task success.** In “Common Conceptions” and its critique, Chaiklin challenges readings that make ZPD a generic interval between easy and difficult tasks. His reconstruction concerns changes in psychological functions and their relations, not simply a larger list of tasks completed with help.
2. **Assistance needs an explanatory role.** The fact that help improves performance is insufficient to identify a developmental process. Ask what becomes possible through collaboration and how that observation relates to the theory being used. A more competent helper alone does not establish a ZPD.
3. **Potential is not an unlimited personal capacity.** Chaiklin relates assisted performance to functions still developing. The concept does not promise effortless learning, enjoyment, or success on any chosen instructional content.
4. **Social and historical setting matters.** In the theoretical-perspective sections, development involves the whole child and a changing relation to culturally organized activity. Chaiklin distinguishes an objective developmental relation associated with an age period from an individual child's current position relative to it. These are his analytical distinctions, not game difficulty measurements.
5. **Imitation means more than repeating movements.** In the assessment discussion, Chaiklin reads Vygotsky's imitation as participation requiring some grasp of task relationships. Copying an observed sequence does not by itself establish the relevant understanding.
6. **Assessment procedures remain incomplete.** Chaiklin finds a theoretical rationale for collaborative assessment but not a fully worked-out universal procedure. He also identifies questions about mental age, imitation and the assumed relation between independent performance and developed functions.

## Why this source matters

The concluding comment explicitly distinguishes ZPD from assisted instruction and scaffolding. It warns against giving ordinary instructional support a developmental-theory label without the corresponding explanatory account. This is a substantive countercheck to simplified accounts of ZPD as any supported challenge.

For this repository, these notes establish conceptual limits only. They do not diagnose a player, infer mastery from level links, or validate any example game's teaching route. Independent transfer after help would require separate observations; it is not established by the framework name.
