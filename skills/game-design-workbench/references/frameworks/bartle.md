# MUD participation patterns

Pack: `bartle` · version 1
Attribution: Richard A. Bartle, Hearts, Clubs, Diamonds, Spades: Players Who Suit MUDs (1996)

## Scope

Use historically situated player/world and acting/interacting distinctions to question activities and tensions in multiplayer designs.

Use only when this lens is selected. Follow [review operation](../review.md); do not repair source during review.

## Inputs and query support

Roles: `mechanics`, `dynamics`, `challenges`, `decisions`, `observations`. Read actual role mapping and live schema/usage before writing.

Available recipes: `query mda`; `query decisions`; `query evidence`; `query records --collection <mapped-alias>`. These are declared-data projections, not automated framework verdicts. Inspect linked bodies for conditions and evidence.

Vocabulary: Achievers, Explorers, Socialisers, Killers describe approaches discussed in multiplayer MUD research; they are not universal identities.

## Original prompts

1. Which activities concern changing the world, understanding it, interacting with people, or exerting influence over them?
2. What makes these activities depend on other participants rather than only non-player systems?
3. Could one encouraged activity prevent another participant from enjoying a different activity?
4. What consent, recovery, or boundary rule contains harmful conflict where relevant?
5. Can the same person pursue different approaches at different times?
6. Is this historical multiplayer frame useful for the selected game, or would applying it distort the design?

## Missing data and evidence limits

If multiplayer participation is absent, report limited applicability and stop this lens instead of inventing social features.

No universal player typing, percentage allocation, psychological diagnosis, or requirement to serve every category. Do not translate Killers directly into all combat or competitive play without context.

## Fixture application

Solo crate puzzle has limited applicability. Record that boundary; no automatic multiplayer recommendation.
