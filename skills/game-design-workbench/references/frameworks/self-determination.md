# Self-Determination Theory

Pack: `self-determination` · version 1
Attribution: Edward Deci, Richard Ryan, and collaborators

## Scope

Question support for autonomy, competence, and relatedness in a selected activity without inferring an individual psychological state.

Use only when this lens is selected. Follow [review operation](../review.md); do not repair source during review.

## Inputs and query support

Roles: `aesthetics`, `mechanics`, `dynamics`, `challenges`, `hypotheses`, `observations`. Read actual role mapping and live schema/usage before writing.

Available recipes: `query mda`; `query routes`; `query evidence`; `query records --collection <mapped-alias>`. These are declared-data projections, not automated framework verdicts. Inspect linked bodies for conditions and evidence.

## Original prompts

1. What choice is meaningful to the intended experience rather than only cosmetically different?
2. Which constraint helps the player act intentionally, and which might feel controlling?
3. What feedback lets the player understand growing competence without relying only on external rewards?
4. Where can a player demonstrate an approach they chose rather than copy a prescribed sequence?
5. What relationship or sense of connection matters in this scope, if any?
6. Which observed response could challenge the designer’s motivation hypothesis?

## Missing data and evidence limits

Keep motivation hypotheses explicit. No observations means unverified support, not unmet psychological needs.

Autonomy, competence, and relatedness are common concepts here, not mandatory profile records or independent fun-point scores. Mechanics alone do not measure need satisfaction. Solo play does not automatically imply a relatedness defect.

## Fixture application

Compare optional route choice with constrained crate movement. Constraints can support deliberate planning; avoid declaring either autonomy support or frustration proven.
