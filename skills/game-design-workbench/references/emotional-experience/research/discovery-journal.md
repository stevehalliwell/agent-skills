# Emotional-experience vocabulary — discovery journal

## Research frame

- Question: Which source-grounded emotional terms and distinctions support a broad, non-exhaustive game-design vocabulary describing who feels what, toward whom/what, and under which appraisals or eliciting situations?
- Mode: orientation. Consumer: shared workbench reference and original framing procedure, not a new framework, validated instrument, or literature-review conclusion.
- Coverage lenses: personal achievement and setbacks; relationships/other-directed feelings; threat/loss/discovery/pleasure; prospective versus actual outcomes and mixed feelings; emotion versus expression, motivation, and absorption.
- Sources: existing retained Lazzaro report/deck plus a bounded primary-source discovery pass on appraisal and emotion vocabulary. Prefer author/institutional material and accessible primary studies. At most eight distinct external source URLs per worker; no site crawling, dictionary harvesting, paid access, origin/popularity claims, statistics synthesis, neurochemical claims, or universal taxonomy.
- Freshness: not time-sensitive; record editions, source context, and access date. Current physical acquisition date is not evidence of publication date.
- Sufficient for this draft: several complementary directly inspectable sources with explicit term/meaning support, useful distinctions, and documented population/model limits. A suggested term without definition support stays a lead or is omitted; no padding to reach a count.
- Selected guides: question-research AI-assisted research (AI discovery/interpretation requires traceable sources); historical/archival research (language/terminology and primary-text fidelity, not an origin investigation). Read both before discovery.
- Output bounds: source-candidates.csv and answer-analyst.md are first-pass research artifacts; final vocabulary definitions, grouping, target/appraisal notes, and play examples are independently authored adaptations. No full retained-evidence workflow or new capture batch authorized by this frame.
