# skill-atoms

Describe learned understanding or a contextual way to apply abilities, not only a verb. Multiple atoms may refer to one game rule; do not split that rule merely because players achieve its conditions in different ways.

Prerequisites are necessary abilities combined as an AND set. Derive unlocks from reverse prerequisite links; never maintain a duplicate unlock list. Alternative methods can be separate atoms sharing understanding and adding different prerequisites. No declared prerequisites does not mean already learned.

Bodies distinguish understanding, expression, learning loop and evidence sought. Evidence sought is not observed mastery. Shared prerequisite/mechanic links can suggest comparisons, not prove contrast or transfer. Keep teaching sequence in challenge routes.

Rank is conceptual first-required placement, not prerequisite depth or physical level. Hypothetical known selections are transient assumptions: do not infer prerequisites, automatic learning or authored rank changes.
