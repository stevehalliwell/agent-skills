## Behavior and epistemic status

Predicted: a retry gives players another chance to change launch timing, route or contact choice.

## Support and limitations

Reset and checkpoint reduce some repetition but do not prove learning. Repeated deaths may indicate misunderstanding, poor feedback or inaccessible execution. Retained score/replenished rewards can also produce farming rather than practice.
