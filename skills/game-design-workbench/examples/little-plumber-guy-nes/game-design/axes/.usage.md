# axes

Reusable independent state/quantity definition. Actors reference axes through noun `axes`; interaction context can reuse definitions without making them actor state. One record lists known named values; no per-state records or mandatory role taxonomy.

- `value_type`: unspecified, enum, integer or number. Unknown type/unit stays unknown; do not invent missing values.
- Enum: `allowed_values` lists known nonempty strings. Open by default; `closed: true` closes only this vocabulary. Preserve duplicate declarations for diagnosis; do not infer reachable cross-axis combinations.
- Numeric: declare `unit`; use `1` for explicit dimensionless quantities. Optional `minimum`/`maximum` are literal inclusive constraints, not initial/current values or gameplay clamp/wrap/reject behavior. Share native units/safe-integer rules. Numeric axes do not take named values or `closed: true`.
- Axis does not own global controls, actor instances, resource flows or transitions. Keep initial/capacity controls in their existing owners; rules own conditional effects.
- Body explains meaning, existing rule sources and material unknowns. Preserve noun identity across state changes. Loading an axis alone does not establish applicability.
- Default status proposed; accepted means adopted definition, not observed play evidence. Preserve accepted source rules and open questions during representation changes. No automatic promotion or repair.

Inspect with native `gd rules --axes axes=game-design/axes --nouns nouns=game-design/nouns` plus explicit interaction/mechanic mappings. Readiness flags do not prove gameplay validity. See installed skill's `native/axes.md` for grammar and current analysis limits.
