## Meaning

Known `small`, `tall` and `spark` forms of the same [hero](../nouns/hero.md). [Growth](../mechanics/growth.md), [spark-upgrade](../mechanics/spark-upgrade.md) and [enemy-contact](../mechanics/enemy-contact.md) own changes. Form is independent of life and protection, not a separate player entity or retry budget. Open vocabulary does not establish every cross-axis combination as reachable.
