## Intended experience

Player feels a formerly awkward sequence has become deliberate through understanding and execution.

## Support and limits

Related challenges and retries could make improvement noticeable. Automatic success, unreadable failure or an unexplained capability change could undermine that feeling. Finishing the course is not itself evidence of felt competence.
