## Intended experience

Player wonders what an unusual block, upper shelf or pipe might offer and elects to investigate.

## Support and limits

Optional rewards and capability changes can invite discovery. Invisible affordances may instead produce aimless guessing; clock pressure can discourage exploration. Curiosity is desired, not inferred from a clicked or entered route.
