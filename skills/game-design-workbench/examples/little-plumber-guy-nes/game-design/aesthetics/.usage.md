# aesthetics

One intended felt experience or experiential quality at MDA's aesthetics layer, not the overall player experience or player story. Name the intended feeling directly in plain language or a supplied term; an MDA category label is optional. Use the workbench's shared emotion vocabulary for meanings and distinctions, and emotional-experience guidance for targets, mixtures, or transitions. These are authoring aids, not required fields or a fixed taxonomy.

Rules–Behaviours–Feelings is a plain-language bridge: relevant mechanics define rules, dynamics describe possible behavior during play, and aesthetics describes how that could feel. Cite supporting rules/behaviors rather than treat their existence as proof of a feeling. Distinguish intention, prediction, and reported experience.

Compare with project experience brief; record support, contradiction, and drift without equating the two lenses.
