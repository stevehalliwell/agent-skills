## Intended experience

After the final landing, clear success feedback lets player release attention to hazards and time.

## Support and limits

A visible endpoint and stopped clock can support relief. An ambiguous finish or anticlimactic route could fail to do so. This is intended transition, not a scripted account of anyone's emotions.
