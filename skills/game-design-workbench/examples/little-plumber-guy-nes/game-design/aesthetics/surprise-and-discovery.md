## Intended experience

An optional entrance reveals a contrasting safe room and a new route back into the course.

## Support and limits

Unexpected space can reward self-directed exploration. A transfer without readable context might instead disorient. Discovering the cellar is neither mandatory nor proof of a reported feeling.
