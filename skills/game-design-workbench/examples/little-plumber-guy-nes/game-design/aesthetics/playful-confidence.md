## Intended experience

Player feels willing to try another action because early safe space and readable changes suggest mistakes can be understood.

## Support and limits

Forward progress and retry opportunity could support the concept's approachable start. First enemy before well-established jumping may instead create anxiety. Intention only; no participant report or measured confidence.
