## Intended experience

Player notices stakes around a gap, returning shell or dwindling clock while retaining a sense that a considered action can help.

## Support and limits

Power to preserve and limited time support possible tension. Excessively tight timing or unclear shell state could create helplessness instead. Desired level is qualitative, not an aggregate tension score.
