## Arrangement

Small, tall and spark form change capability and damage consequence. Pickups can upgrade; unprotected enemy contact returns powered forms to small with brief grace. Shooting additionally needs a free live-shot slot. Capability progression is not a numeric health bar or a proof of increasing player competence.
