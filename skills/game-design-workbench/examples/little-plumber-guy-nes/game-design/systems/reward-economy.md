## Arrangement

Blocks release consumables or direct coin payouts. Coin bank can convert to lives, while points remain non-spendable feedback. Brick destruction changes space as well as score. Replenished retry layouts permit repeated collection; balancing that farming opportunity is an open design question, not hidden anti-farming logic.
