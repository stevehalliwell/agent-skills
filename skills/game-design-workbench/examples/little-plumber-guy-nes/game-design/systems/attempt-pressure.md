## Arrangement

Clock bounds active attempt time; lives bound repeated lethal failures. Earned lives and checkpoint placement can soften that pressure without removing gaps or contact hazards. Resource constraints create possible trade-offs, not a computed frustration or difficulty score. No empirical balance claim.
