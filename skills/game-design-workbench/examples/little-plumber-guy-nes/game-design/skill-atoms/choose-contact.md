## Learning loop

Read threat and current form; choose avoidance, stomp or available ranged action.

## Understanding and expression

Not every contact is safe and not every form supports every response. Authored prerequisites require both stomp knowledge and power reading.

## Evidence sought

Ask why a response was selected. Route access alone cannot prove that decision skill.
