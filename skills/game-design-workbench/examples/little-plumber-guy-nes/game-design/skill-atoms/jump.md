## Learning loop

Press jump from support, see ascent and landing, then combine with forward input.

## Understanding and expression

Grounded launch clears an obstacle; repeated air input is not a second jump.

## Evidence sought

Player can choose when to launch. First-walker introduces this within a threat, so safe prior teaching is not guaranteed.
