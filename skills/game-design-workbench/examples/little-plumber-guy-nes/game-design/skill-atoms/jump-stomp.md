## Learning loop

Choose a launch, aim descent onto the walker, inspect the contact, and adjust the next attempt.

## Understanding

Combine above-impact understanding with intentional target landing. Both prerequisites are necessary for this authored method; the game rule itself does not require a jump.

## Expression

Jump and align a descending top impact. `land-on-target` carries the launch and approach prerequisites; do not duplicate that whole chain here.

## Evidence sought

Repeated intentional jump-based impacts and an explanation of why side contact differs. The first-walker encounter offers an opportunity, not proof of mastery.
