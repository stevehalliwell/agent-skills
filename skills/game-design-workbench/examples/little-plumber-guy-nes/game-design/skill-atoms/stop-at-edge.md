## Learning loop

Approach a visible boundary on safe ground and release movement before passing it.

## Understanding and expression

Movement must be stopped deliberately; lack of support is dangerous. Express controlled approach, not random hesitation.

## Evidence sought

Observe repeated intentional stopping. Actual braking physics must be specified before numerical stopping-distance claims.
