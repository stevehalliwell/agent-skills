## Learning loop

Read a support edge, choose departure and horizontal alignment, observe descent, then revise positioning.

## Understanding

A fall can place the hero below the starting support without a jump launch. Controlled approach supports choosing where to leave the edge.

## Expression

Leave support intentionally and align descent with a target. This is an authored skill proposal, not a new movement rule or proof that a specific geometry is reachable.

## Evidence sought

Repeated intentional alignment from an edge, without relying on a jump to start descent.
