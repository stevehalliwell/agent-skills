## Learning loop

Predict a fall from a support, align with the walker below, inspect the impact and revise the approach.

## Understanding

Combine the same above-impact understanding with controlled falling. Jump launch is not a prerequisite for this method.

## Expression

Leave a ledge without launching a jump and align a descending top impact. This refers to the existing walker-stomp rule, not a second rule for falls.

## Evidence sought

Intentional fall-based impact and explanation of why the rule still applies without a jump.
