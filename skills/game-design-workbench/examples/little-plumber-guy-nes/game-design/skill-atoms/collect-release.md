## Learning loop

Release a cap, follow its movement and touch it; inspect resulting form.

## Understanding and expression

A moving object can be a reward rather than an enemy. Bumping the supply is not sufficient to gain power.

## Evidence sought

Observe deliberate pursuit without assuming the reward's appearance is already legible.
