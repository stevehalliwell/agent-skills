## Learning loop

Approach, launch and steer onto raised support; revise after overshoot or undershoot.

## Understanding and expression

Jump timing and horizontal placement jointly determine landing. Prerequisites are an AND set: jumping plus controlled approach.

## Evidence sought

Repeated intentional landings, not one accidental success. No declared challenge can certify physical reachability.
