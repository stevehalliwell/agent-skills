# Editorial review

Revise supplied long-form prose without replacing its authorial intent.

## Workflow

1. Establish editorial brief. Identify supplied draft, target channel, intended reader, desired outcome, author voice, and available factual sources. Ask for missing information that materially changes revision; otherwise state assumptions.
2. Edit complete draft. Improve structure, meaning, phrasing, pacing, clarity, grammar, title, opening, links, calls to action, positioning language, and reader context where relevant to agreed channel. Preserve author intent and voice unless they conflict with clarity, accuracy, or agreed channel fit.
3. Handle claims. Correct claims only from supplied sources. Flag uncertain or unsupported claims rather than inventing support.
4. Return revision. Provide revised draft and concise material-change rationale, including meaningful trade-offs.

## Rules

- Work on supplied long-form drafts only.
- Do not create first drafts, develop marketing conversion strategy, perform autonomous research, or publish.
- Keep editorial judgment separate from factual approval.
