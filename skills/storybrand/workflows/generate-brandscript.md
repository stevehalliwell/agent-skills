# Generate a BrandScript

## Required read

Read [../references/storybrand-framework.md](../references/storybrand-framework.md). Done when the framework and claim limits are known.

## Workflow

1. Choose the script scope—brand, division, offer, campaign, or other coherent decision context—then gather its offer, audience, primary customer desire, desired action, evidence, constraints, customer language, and existing positioning. Use separate scripts when audiences, offers, or desired outcomes materially differ. Record supplied local-source path, origin or permission note, and claim boundary. Label unsupported statements `Assumption` or `Question`. Done when scope is explicit and every script field has supported input, an assumption, or a question.
2. For file output, ask for a target path before writing; chat output needs no path. Done when output mode is known.
3. Produce the BrandScript in the shape below. Preserve confirmed facts and make gaps explicit. Done when all applicable sections are completed or marked as gaps.
4. Read [../references/post-storybrand-checks.md](../references/post-storybrand-checks.md) and run every check, marking genuinely inapplicable checks as such. Apply supported remedies or record them as next work. Done when the BrandScript includes a post-check result for every check.

## Output shape

```markdown
# BrandScript: <offer or organisation>

## Sources
- <public URL or supplied source> — <what it supports>

## Script scope
- <brand, division, offer, campaign, or other decision context>
- **Primary customer desire:** <one tangible desire for this scope>

## Confirmed facts
- <fact and source>

## Assumptions
- <unverified working assumption>

## Open questions
- <missing evidence or decision>

## 1. Character / hero
- **Who is this hero?** <one clearly defined customer or customer type who recognises themselves immediately>
- **What is their quest?** <one specific, tangible goal the customer actively seeks>

## 2. Problem
- **Villain / root cause (optional):** <named circumstance, system, or friction; never an invented enemy>
- **External problem:** <practical situation, task, barrier, or missing capability>
- **Internal problem:** <credible frustration, fear, uncertainty, or effect the external problem creates>
- **Philosophical problem:** <why this situation should not be acceptable>
- **Connected problem:** <how the three levels connect>

## 3. Guide
- **How does the organisation serve the journey?** <how the organisation guides or equips the hero, without competing for the limelight>
- **Empathy:** <customer-centred acknowledgement grounded in supplied context>
- **Authority:** <relevant supported evidence, experience, process, credential, or an explicit evidence gap>

## 4. Plan
- **Process plan:** <short customer-facing path; normally three action-led steps, or honest grouped phases>
- **Agreement plan (optional):** <approved promise, policy, standard, or guarantee that reduces a relevant fear>
- **Paradigm plan (optional):** <supported change in how the customer understands the problem>

## 5. Call to action
- **Direct CTA:** <plain primary conversion action and its honest commitment>
- **Transitional CTA:** <genuine lower-commitment step that provides value>

## 6. Failure stakes
- **What continues or worsens if the customer does nothing?** <credible practical or emotional consequence linked to the problem>

## 7. Success outcome
- **What has changed for the customer?** <tangible outcome after completing the plan or using the offer>
- **How does that outcome feel or enable their goal?** <concrete, supported customer-centred benefit>
- **Character transformation (optional):** from <current state> → to <better state>; <evidence or explicit gap>

## One-liner
- **Problem:** <customer inside a specific problem>
- **Solution:** <plain, differentiating solution>
- **Reward:** <supported better state>
- **Draft:** <customer> struggles with <problem>. <offer> provides <solution>, so they can <reward>.
- **Clarity check:** <read aloud; test with someone unfamiliar with the offer; record whether they identify audience, offer, value, and next logical question>

## Post-StoryBrand checks
- **Distinctiveness and voice:** <good | needs work | not supplied> — <finding and remedy>
- **Complex buying:** <not applicable | good | needs work> — <finding and remedy>
- **Individual or collective success:** <good | needs work> — <finding and remedy>
- **Ethical stakes:** <good | needs work> — <finding and remedy>
- **Enough information:** <not applicable | good | needs work> — <finding and remedy>
- **Asset role:** <good | needs work> — <asset job, relevant story elements, and next action>

## Next use
- <adapt source material into the chosen channel or talking points; record what requires validation next>
```
