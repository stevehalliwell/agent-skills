# StoryBrand

StoryBrand is a messaging framework that makes the customer the character, positions the organisation as a credible guide, and makes the desired action clear. Use it when an offer or piece of content is hard to understand quickly, puts the brand ahead of the customer, has an unclear next step, or needs a coherent message across channels. It is not evidence for product, customer, outcome, or authority claims.

## Workflow selection

Identify the user's intended outcome, then read **only** its workflow:

- **Generate a BrandScript** — create a first script from an offer, audience, evidence, and goals. Read [workflows/generate-brandscript.md](workflows/generate-brandscript.md).
- **Review or analyse an existing brand or content** — inspect an existing BrandScript, website, copy, pamphlet, or other material; compare what it communicates with the user's intended StoryBrand; identify gaps; and, when requested, provide direction, a rewrite, a draft, or a revised BrandScript. Read [workflows/review-brandscript.md](workflows/review-brandscript.md).

If the requested outcome is ambiguous, ask whether they want to generate a BrandScript or review/analyse existing material. Done when one workflow is selected.
