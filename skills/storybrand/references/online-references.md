# StoryBrand online references

Use these links to check framework terminology and extend the skill's reference material. They guide StoryBrand method only; they do not support claims about a user's product, customers, authority, or outcomes.

## Official StoryBrand sources

- [Your Brand Is Not the Hero](https://storybrand.com/downloads/intro-to-sb/Introduction-to-StoryBrand.pdf) — concise SB7 overview: customer as hero, brand as guide, problem levels, guide, plan, stakes, and clarity.
- [StoryBrand Online Marketing Course Workbook](https://storybrand.com/downloads/StoryBrand-Online-Marketing-Course-Workbook.pdf) — detailed classic BrandScript prompts, including villain, plans, CTAs, and success/failure.
- [Building a StoryBrand Audio Book Graphics Reference](https://storybrand.com/downloads/basb/Building-a-StoryBrand-Audio-Book-Images.pdf) — compact BrandScript field map, including process/agreement plans and character transformation.
- [5 Things Your Website Should Include](https://storybrand.com/downloads/5-Things-Your-Website-Should-Include.pdf) — website-specific clarity, CTA, internal-problem, and simplified-offer guidance.
- [StoryBrand Staff Confusion Fix-It Kit](https://storybrand.com/downloads/StoryBrand-Staff-Confusion-Fix-It-Kit.pdf) — one-liner exercise: Problem → Solution → Reward, plus team message alignment.
- [StoryBrand Soundbite Strategy training](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5OJT1ph-yL4) — PEACE soundbites (Problem, Empathy, Answer, Change, End Result) and Curiosity → Enlightenment → Commitment campaign phases.

## Independent implementation and critique

- [StoryBrand implementation mistakes — Evan Cox](https://evancoxconsulting.com/when-implementing-the-storybrand-framework-avoid-these-common-mistakes/) — practitioner advice on preserving voice, treating the BrandScript as talking points, and choosing one primary problem.
- [StoryBrand agreement plan — Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/blog/storybrand-agreement-plan/) — secondary explanation of process and agreement plans.
- [StoryBrand method drawbacks — Column](https://columncontent.com/storybrand-method-drawbacks/) — critical questions about formulaic messaging, differentiation, multi-stakeholder B2B, and founder voice.
