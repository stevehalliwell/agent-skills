# Curiosity → Enlightenment → Commitment

This is an optional campaign-deployment sequence from current StoryBrand soundbite training. It helps decide what a customer needs at different levels of readiness. It is not a replacement for the SB7 BrandScript, customer research, channel strategy, accessibility, legal review, or measurement.

Source: [StoryBrand Soundbite Strategy training transcript](sources/storybrand-soundbite-strategy-transcript-prose.md).

## 1. Curiosity

Earn initial attention with short, easily understood problem-focused messaging.

- **Customer state:** may not yet be ready to hear a detailed pitch.
- **Job:** make a relevant problem and possible answer recognisable.
- **Source material:** the PEACE problem, empathy, and answer statements; the BrandScript's hero and problem.
- **Check:** can someone quickly recognise the problem and want to know more?

## 2. Enlightenment

Give interested customers the information needed to understand the offer and judge fit.

- **Customer state:** has granted attention and needs clarity before acting.
- **Job:** explain how the offer, process, cost, risk, proof, and fit work at the level appropriate to the channel.
- **Source material:** the BrandScript's guide, authority, process/agreement plan, and one-liner; approved evidence only.
- **Check:** does the material answer the questions needed for an informed next step without hiding obligations or inventing proof?

## 3. Commitment

Invite a ready customer to take the direct action.

- **Customer state:** understands the offer and needs a clear, proportionate decision path.
- **Job:** make the direct CTA, commitment, conditions, and any genuine incentive clear.
- **Source material:** the BrandScript's direct CTA, plan, credible stakes, and success outcome.
- **Check:** is the ask specific, honest about commitment, accessible, and supported by the organisation's actual process?

## Campaign sequence

For each channel or asset, state its primary stage, intended audience readiness, message source, approved claims, next action, and evidence gap. Do not force every asset through all three stages: a short ad may only create curiosity, while a proposal may primarily support enlightenment and commitment.

Use the sequence only after the underlying SB7 story is coherent and evidence-bound. Validate it against actual audience behaviour rather than assuming the sequence will improve attention, confidence, or conversion.
