# Business Viability Research

Use this reference when the question asks whether an activity, product, market, or side business is viable. It supports an evidence dossier and decision support; it cannot establish guaranteed revenue or personal fit.

## Coverage lenses

Select only the lenses that answer the focused question:

- **Market definition:** product, buyer, geography, platform, and period.
- **Supply and competition:** catalogue size, active sellers, pricing, positioning, and differentiation.
- **Demand signals:** direct sales/rank data where available, reviews, buyer questions, and credible reporting. Treat interest and anecdotes as signals, not prevalence or sales proof.
- **Operating reality:** public operator evidence about support, maintenance, acquisition, platform rules, fulfilment, and workload.
- **Economics:** observed prices, fees, costs, refunds, and other inputs; distinguish gross sale price from net proceeds.
- **Constraints and risks:** policy or approval requirements, platform dependence, support obligations, legal constraints, and missing information.

Seek primary marketplace/platform data, buyer evidence, and operator experience alongside one another. A large catalogue, positive reviews, or one seller’s report alone does not establish opportunity viability.

## Scenario modelling

Use models only to help user judgement. Retain the source or rationale for every non-user assumption and label the output `derived scenario`, not observed earnings.

For each scenario show:

- formula, time period, and currency;
- units sold or clients acquired per period;
- price and platform/payment fees;
- costs, refunds, tax treatment, and support time when included;
- low/base/high assumptions; and
- assumptions that dominate the result or lack evidence.

Do not infer sales volume from review counts unless the conversion relationship is evidenced and its limits are stated.

## Scope guard

Comparable products or channels can be proposed as follow-up questions. Do not add them to the corpus unless the user approves broadening the inquiry.
