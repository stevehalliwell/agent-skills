# Current Data and Trend Research

Use this reference when the question depends on conditions that can change: rankings, popularity, prices, availability, catalogue state, usage metrics, or a recent trend.

## Coverage lenses

Define the metric before collecting it:

- **Measure:** the exact value, ranking rule, unit, population, geography, and time window.
- **Time:** whether the question means a current snapshot, a recent period, or a historical comparison; record observation time and source refresh cadence where visible.
- **Primary observation:** the relevant first-party or direct data source.
- **Corroboration:** independent/direct sources where practical, especially if metric definitions or figures disagree.
- **Context:** reporting and commentary explaining changes, clearly kept separate from current observations.
- **Limits:** access, sampling, update lag, definitional differences, and unobservable data.

## Discovery and capture

In the first-blush source brief, require primary/live data candidates as well as explanatory sources. Do not let a monthly roundup, article, or search snippet stand in for a current measurement.

Rendered stores, marketplaces, dashboards, and rankings are Crawl4AI candidates. Reconnoitre the base page and then capture only explicit URLs or an approved enumerated sample. Retain the page plus a CSV/JSON extract when figures, rank, price, or catalogue fields matter.

For each volatile observation retain: source URL, metric definition, observed value, observation/capture time, and extraction limits. Record disagreement rather than silently combining values.

## Synthesis

State the conclusion as of the capture window. Separate:

- what was directly observed;
- what reported or expert sources say;
- what comparison or interpretation was derived; and
- what could have changed after capture.
