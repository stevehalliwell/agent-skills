# Question Research Reference Catalogue

Use this catalogue during inquiry framing. Select every guide whose trigger applies, record the selection and reason in the research register, and read only the selected guides before discovery.

| Guide | Select when the inquiry includes | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`current-data.md`](current-data.md) | rankings, popularity, prices, availability, catalogue state, usage metrics, or recent trends | Define volatile metrics and retain time-bound observations. |
| [`business-viability.md`](business-viability.md) | market opportunity, product or side-business viability, pricing, earnings, operating burden, or competition | Build bounded decision support without treating signals as proof of demand or earnings. |

When adding a new branch guide, add it to this table with concrete selection triggers and a one-line purpose. Do not select a guide solely because its topic is adjacent.
